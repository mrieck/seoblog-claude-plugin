# SEO research

## Topic selection

**Keyword mode** — the user supplied keywords:
- First keyword = **primary** (title, first paragraph, at least one H2).
- Remaining keywords = secondary/LSI — woven naturally into ONE article, not one
  article each.
- Research all of them together to find a cohesive angle.

**Auto-discover mode** — no keywords given:
1. Review existing content (state history + live article list) for coverage and gaps.
2. WebSearch for: trending topics in the niche; "People Also Ask" questions;
   competitor content the site hasn't covered; seasonal/timely opportunities.
3. Select a topic that is NOT already covered, complements existing content
   (extends a topical cluster or starts a new one), shows real search interest,
   and genuinely helps readers in this niche.

## Research steps (per topic)

1. **SERP analysis** — WebSearch the primary keyword. From the top results:
   what subtopics do they cover, what depth is typical, what questions are
   answered?
2. **Competitor deep-dive** — WebFetch 2–3 top-ranking articles: content
   structure (headings, flow), unique angles and data points, what's missing or
   could be done better.
3. **Related/LSI keywords** — WebSearch variations:
   - "[keyword] tips"
   - "[keyword] best practices"
   - "[keyword] vs [alternative]"
   - "how to [keyword]"
4. **Find your angle** — what will this article do differently? More actionable
   advice, more current information, a unique perspective from the site's
   niche, or better organization/comprehensiveness.
5. **Product research** (only if the site config has a `product`) — WebFetch the
   product URL and understand features, audience, and value proposition; note
   where the product genuinely relates to the topic. If the keyword has no
   natural connection to the product, plan a great article without forcing it.

## Quality bar

If research shows the topic is too thin to support a substantive article, or
it's already well covered on the site, skip it and choose another. Never write
filler to satisfy a keyword.
